Case 01 — E-commerce DTC

Tough Glove — Welcome & Discount Flow

3-part welcome sequence · brand story · social proof
+
Brief

Client request: Build a 3-part welcome sequence to onboard new subscribers, deliver a 10% discount (WELCOME10), communicate brand durability, share founder Omar's story, and highlight the best-selling Gator Gripz with social proof.
Audience: Mechanics, tradesmen, and industrial workers dealing with harsh chemicals, grease, and heavy tools.

Email 1 — Welcome & discount delivery
  • A Welcome to Tough Glove
  • B You're Officially Part of Tough Glove
  • C Glad You're Here at Tough Glove

Open up to claim your special 10% off welcome gift

Instant delivery of the WELCOME10 code, leading with heavy-duty protection against grease and chemicals.

Email 2 — Brand story & founder message
  • A Why we built Tough Glove
  • B A personal message from our founder
  • C The real story behind our heavy duty gear

Find out how we created a stronger glove for tradesmen.

Storytelling around founder Omar in a gritty workshop, watching mechanics rip cheap gloves 5–6 times a shift — leading into the 12mil-thick nitrile solution.

Email 3 — Hero product & reviews
  • A Our most popular gloves sell out fast
  • B Meet the Tough Glove best sellers
  • C The #1 heavy duty gloves on the job site

See why thousands of workers trust this glove.

Showcasing Gator Gripz — trusted by 50,000+ workers, touchscreen-compatible, backed by 5-star reviews.

Case 03 — Coaching

Love At First Fight — Relationship Welcome Sequence

4-part nurture · opt-in delivery · soft pitch
+
Brief

Client request: A 4-part welcome sequence for relationship coach Bruce Muzik after a free video opt-in ("The Three Relationship Killers") — build rapport, confirm deliverability, explain the root cause of relationship breakdown, and soft-pitch the 6-week "Conflict Cure" program.
Audience: Couples in constant friction, silent treatment, or arguing, looking for practical tools to reconnect.

Email 1 — Free video delivery

Delivers the training, sets expectations, and establishes 25+ years of experience with 10,000+ couples.

Email 2 — Engagement check-in

Plain-text, conversational ask to confirm the link worked — helps bypass spam filters and prompts replies.

Email 3 — Core framework

Subject: Why talking is not always the answer

One idea: communication fails when emotional connection is lost. One problem: couples use silence to avoid fights. One solution: a 3-step system to break silence safely.

Email 4 — Case study & soft pitch

Subject: How one couple stopped the constant drama

A couple spent 6 months in silence and lost physical warmth — resolved with specific scripts from The Conflict Cure program.
